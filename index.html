How to Play

Goal: Be the first to bring all 4 tokens to the goal.

Starting: Roll a combined 7 or doubles to leave home base.

Movement: Move one token by the two-dice total. Doubles grant an extra roll.

Captures: Land on an opponent to send them home. Safe zones protect tokens.

Challenges: Every 10 moves a random player gets a skippable challenge. Never mandatory.

Win: First to get all 4 tokens to goal wins!
